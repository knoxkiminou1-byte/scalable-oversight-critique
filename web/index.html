Resistance to a Corrupted Critique

Does a weak/cheap judge defer to a confidently wrong critique? We hand-fabricated 7 fake, plausible-sounding "expert" arguments for the wrong verdict and fed them to the judge alongside its own reasoning.

Accuracy by condition

All five conditions — including judgment after seeing the corrupted critique — landed at the same accuracy.

The 7 corrupted items, in detail

Each of these had a fabricated critique confidently arguing for the wrong verdict, with an invented-sounding justification. The judge's final verdict matched ground truth on all 7 anyway.
